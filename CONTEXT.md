# Personal Page

Jonas Knüppel’s canonical personal positioning website. It introduces Jonas, communicates his current interests and work, and routes visitors to Kernscale and selected public profiles.

## Language

**Attention**:
A scarce human and market resource that can be earned, directed, and converted into perception, trust, and demand.

**Attention system**:
A repeatable combination of positioning, content, distribution, design, outreach, and measurement.
_Avoid_: Marketing trick, decorative concept

**Digital persuasion**:
Deliberate messaging, design, interaction, and distribution that influence perception without implying deception or coercion.

**Digital presence**:
A person or organization’s outward presentation across websites, social channels, and content.
_Avoid_: Digital appearance, when writing conventional public copy

**Organic intelligence**:
Human judgment grounded in psychology, culture, lived experience, and naturally developed insight.
_Avoid_: Artificial intelligence

## Relationships

- The personal page expresses the **Attention** thesis through Jonas’s **Digital presence**.
- Kernscale applies **Attention systems** commercially.
- **Digital persuasion** is one mechanism inside an **Attention system**.

## Boundaries

Biographical facts, roles, education, and current activities are time-sensitive. Do not invent private facts, credentials, results, age, or accomplishments.

Detailed product, content, design, SEO, and technical history is preserved in [docs/product-context-legacy.md](docs/product-context-legacy.md) and should be read only when the task needs it.
